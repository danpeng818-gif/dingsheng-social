Privacy Policy

Application: Dingsheng Social Analytics

Effective Date: October 19, 2026

1. Introduction

Dingsheng Social Analytics is an internal social media analytics application designed to help authorized users manage and analyze social media account performance.

This Privacy Policy explains how information is accessed, used, stored, and protected when using our application.

2. Information We Collect

With your authorization and subject to available API permissions, we may collect:

  • TikTok account identifiers and profile information.
  • Publicly available video information.
  • Video view counts.
  • Video likes, comments, and share counts.
  • Account follower statistics, where permitted.
  • Account authorization tokens required for API access.
  • Performance data manually imported by authorized users.

3. How We Use Information

We use authorized account data for the following purposes:

  • Monitoring social media account performance.
  • Generating daily and historical reports.
  • Analyzing video engagement and content performance.
  • Supporting internal social media operations.
  • Maintaining authorized API connections.

4. TikTok Account Authorization

Access to TikTok account data requires authorization through TikTok's official authorization process.

We request only the permissions necessary to provide the application's supported features. Users may revoke authorization through TikTok's available account settings.

5. Data Storage and Security

The application is designed to store reporting data in a local application database. Access to stored information is intended to be limited to authorized users.

We take reasonable measures to protect information against unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration, or loss.

No method of electronic storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

6. Data Sharing

We do not sell personal information. Authorized data is used for internal reporting and application functionality.

Information may be disclosed where required by applicable law or where necessary to operate authorized platform integrations.

7. Data Retention

Reporting information is retained only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this Privacy Policy, subject to applicable legal and platform requirements.

Authorization credentials are retained only while required to maintain permitted connections.

8. Data Deletion and User Rights

Users may request access to or deletion of information associated with their authorized accounts by contacting us.

Upon receiving a verified request, we will review and process it in accordance with applicable legal requirements and relevant platform policies.

Revoking TikTok authorization stops future authorized API access, but does not necessarily delete previously stored reporting data. Users may separately request deletion of that information.

9. Third-Party Services

The application may integrate with TikTok and other social media platforms. These services operate under their own privacy policies and terms.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy to reflect changes in application functionality, data practices, or legal requirements.

Any updates will be published on this page with a revised effective date.
